`RichSelect` can carry toggles, actions and an inline trigger.

`toggles` are settings that scope the choice (*Next ask only*), drawn below the
options and keeping the menu open; `actions` are acts drawn last (*Manage
worlds…*); `appearance="inline"` is the borderless, compact trigger a toolbar
uses, with `triggerIcon` and `triggerTag`. An option may carry
`disabledReason`, shown under it when it is disabled.
