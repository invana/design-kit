The assistant's composer controls open upward, group their options under a heading (`group`), hint and disable them, take a host-held `value`, and are held shut while a run is live. An answer carries a `report` — the run in counters — which its report icon opens inline under the answer. A step ends its row with a `result`, and its record shows `Show all` for a value that carries its whole text in `full`; a run past 40 steps folds the earlier rows. A proposal step takes `waitingSince` and shows how long it has waited (`humanWait`: `12s`, `1m 2s`, `1h 3m`, `1d 1h`). Patches that arrive within a frame are drawn together.
