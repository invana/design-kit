Annotations have their kit: `AnnotationCard` (an annotation beside its record — its colour, the value it was written against and *Was 71% when noted*, a gap, a session tag, its actions), `AnnotationRow` (one annotation in a list, with its kind, what it is on, who wrote it and its marks) and `AuthorMark` (a person's initials or the assistant's star). Each has a story.
