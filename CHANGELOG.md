## [0.0.26] - 2026-09-16

### Features
- **(ui)** `PanelStack` takes instructions, and says what happened — `stackRef`
  (`expand` · `collapse` · `toggle` · `isCollapsed`) opens the drawer a URL names, and
  `onCollapsedChange` reports every open and close, including a drag past `minSize`.
  There is deliberately no `collapsed` prop: the resizable group owns the geometry.

## [0.0.25] - 2026-09-16

### Features
- **(ui)** `TaskGantt` — where a run's time went, one row per task on the run's own clock.
  Takes the trace's own shape (`task_key`, `status`, `started_at`, `finished_at`,
  `duration_ms`); retries draw as segments, a task that never ran draws as an outline, and
  `onSelectTask` makes the rows pickable.
- **(ui)** `ClampedText` — prose that shows its first few lines and offers the rest in place.
  The toggle exists only when there is something behind it, and that is measured rather than
  guessed from a character count.
- **(ui)** `Eyebrow`, the smallest heading.
